01. UX RESEARCH & PERSONAS

Solving cognitive overload during high-speed performance driving.

PROBLEM

Visual Clutter & Driver Distraction

Legacy digital dashboards stuff screens with redundant nested menus, tiny numbers, and distracting skeuomorphic dials. Driver eye-gaze duration away from the track increases safety hazards.

SOLUTION

Adaptive Focal Telematics

AERO HUD dynamically changes color temperature, visual weight, and readout density based on active drive modes. Critical telemetry (speed, G-forces, RPM redline) is highlighted only when relevant.

User Journey Map: High-Speed Track Lap

STAGE 1

Paddock Setup

Driver selects TRACK or DRIFT mode. Cockpit lighting matches vehicle drive mode. Audio synth calibrates.

STAGE 2

Launch Sprint

Launch Control triggers. High-visibility shift lights flash when approaching 8,000 RPM redline.

STAGE 3

Corner Apex

Lateral G-force vector expands on HUD. Glances under 0.3s confirm vehicle balance and tire traction limits.

STAGE 4

Telemetry Review

Post-lap data automatically logs top speed, 0-100 km/h acceleration times, and battery thermals.
